# Product Philosophy

DogEared is built around the idea that a reading product should help people remember, reflect, and discover without making reading feel like performance.

## What DogEared Values

### Calm

The product uses gentle language and restrained metrics. Momentum, streaks, and goals exist to give context, not pressure. Prediction copy avoids risk-heavy language when there is not enough reading history.

### Privacy

Readers control profile visibility, location sharing, activity sharing, discovery, and follow availability. Private profiles are hidden from non-owners. Activity feeds respect profile and activity-sharing preferences.

### Reader-First Design

Core workflows are about saving books, reading books, finishing books, rating books, and reflecting on books. The application avoids features that distract from reading or turn reading into public performance.

### Accessibility

The design system expects visible focus states, keyboard-reachable shelf and activity controls, readable contrast, and mobile-friendly layouts. Controls favor clear labels and predictable behavior.

### Long-Term Memory

DogEared treats shelves, ratings, reviews, finished dates, progress history, and profile context as part of a reader's durable record. Account email changes preserve reading history and social relationships.

### Thoughtful Discovery

Discovery should come from books, authors, genres, topics, shelves, and readers. DogEared favors explicit reader context over opaque engagement ranking.

### Community Without Performance

Following, likes, comments, and activity feeds exist, but the product tone stays closer to a reading journal and community library than a social media feed.

## Things DogEared Intentionally Avoids

- Ads.
- Infinite scrolling as the default reading pattern.
- Engagement algorithms optimized for time-on-site.
- Dark patterns.
- Popularity contests.
- Leaderboards for reading.
- Endless notifications.
- Growth hacks.
- Manipulative urgency.
- Punitive progress language.
- Treating every action as content.
- Making private reading preferences unnecessarily public.

## Product Tradeoffs

DogEared accepts that calmer products may grow more slowly. It prioritizes trust, shelf reliability, and useful memory over constant novelty. Metrics and social features are included only when they support the reader's own goals or help maintain the community.
